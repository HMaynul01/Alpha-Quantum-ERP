Core Features

Everything your business needs

Built for Saudi Arabian businesses with VAT support, SAR currency, and IONOS email integration.

💰
Finance & Expenses
Submit, approve, and track expenses with line items, VAT calculation, receipt uploads, and PDF export. Full approval workflow.
🧾
Invoicing
Create professional invoices, track payment status, manage client records, and generate financial reports automatically.
📊
Budget Tracking
Set budgets by category and project. Track actual vs planned spending with visual progress bars and analytics dashboard.
👷
HR Management
Manage workers, Iqama numbers, attendance, timesheet, and salary payments. Built for Saudi workforce compliance.
📁
Project Management
Track projects from planning to completion. Kanban boards, task assignment, budget allocation, and progress monitoring.
🏢
CRM & Leads
Manage customers and leads pipeline. Track sources, assign staff, monitor conversion rates, and maximize revenue.
🔐
Role-Based Access
Granular permissions per user per module. Creator, Cube Admin, Superuser, and Staff roles. Full audit trail of every action.
🧊
Multi-Tenant Cubes
Each company gets their own isolated Cube with separate data, users, and settings. One platform, infinite businesses.
📱
Mobile First
Fully responsive design optimized for mobile. Submit expenses, approve invoices, and check the dashboard from anywhere.
